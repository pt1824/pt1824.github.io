Contract Law

Clear agreements that protect your interests and effective enforcement when those agreements are breached.

Well-Drafted Contracts Reduce Risk

Business relationships depend on clear expectations. A carefully prepared contract allocates risk, defines performance standards, sets payment terms, and provides mechanisms for resolving disputes. The Law Office of Keval Patel assists clients with the full life cycle of commercial agreements—from initial drafting through negotiation and, when necessary, enforcement.

Drafting & Negotiation

The firm prepares and reviews a wide variety of agreements, including:

  • Sales and procurement contracts
  • Distribution and supply agreements
  • Service and consulting agreements
  • Business process and operations contracts
  • Buy-sell agreements and related ownership documents
  • Custom agreements tailored to unique business needs

The goal is always the same: language that accurately reflects the parties’ deal while protecting the client against foreseeable problems.

Contract Enforcement & Disputes

When the other party fails to perform, prompt and strategic action matters. The firm has prosecuted and defended claims for breach of contract, seeking remedies that may include damages, specific performance, or other appropriate relief under Texas law.

Strong contracts are one of the most cost-effective ways to protect a business. Investing time up front often prevents expensive disputes later.

Who Benefits

Small and mid-sized businesses, entrepreneurs, service providers, and individuals entering significant commercial relationships in the Sugar Land and Houston markets.
