About Keval M. Patel

Civil attorney serving Sugar Land and the greater Houston area with a focus on practical, client-centered representation.

Professional Background

Keval M. Patel is a Texas-licensed attorney based in Sugar Land. He began his career at one of the nation’s top five accounting firms in the Research and Development Tax Credit Group. In that role he performed both consulting and legal work, assisting Fortune 500 companies in quantifying and qualifying R&D activities and representing clients in IRS audits.

After leaving the firm, he established his own practice in the Sugar Land area. Early work included a broad range of matters—civil litigation, tax, probate, contract disputes, real estate, and criminal defense. Over time the practice refined its focus toward real estate, estate planning, tax-related issues, contracts, and commercial disputes.

Today the Law Office of Keval Patel continues that commitment: providing careful analysis, clear communication, and steady advocacy for individuals and businesses throughout Fort Bend County and the Houston metropolitan area.

Admitted to practice: State Bar of Texas (2006), Texas Supreme Court, U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Texas.

Practice Philosophy

Clients come to the firm with real problems that affect their families, homes, and businesses. The approach is straightforward: listen carefully, explain options in plain language, prepare thoroughly, and pursue the resolution that best serves the client’s goals—whether that is a negotiated agreement or formal advocacy in court.

Experience with tax and accounting issues also informs estate planning and business advice, helping clients anticipate secondary consequences that pure litigation experience alone might overlook.

Areas of Focus

While the firm has handled a wide variety of civil matters over the years, current emphasis includes:

  • Estate planning documents and probate administration
  • Residential and commercial real estate transactions and disputes
  • Contract drafting, negotiation, and enforcement
  • Business and commercial litigation for small and mid-sized companies
  • Insurance coverage and claim disputes
