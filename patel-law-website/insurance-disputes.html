Insurance Disputes

Advocacy when an insurance company fails to honor the coverage you paid for.

When Coverage Is Denied or Delayed

Policyholders reasonably expect their insurer to investigate claims promptly and pay amounts owed under the policy. When that does not happen—through delay, underpayment, improper denial, or other unfair practices—legal assistance can help restore the benefits the policy was meant to provide.

The Law Office of Keval Patel assists individuals and businesses confronting insurance-related problems, from initial claim disputes through formal resolution.

Common Issues Addressed

  • Arbitrary changes to coverage or policy terms
  • Failure to provide proper notice of cancellation or material changes
  • Unreasonable delays in investigating or settling claims
  • Denial of coverage that appears available under the policy language
  • Bad-faith conduct, including termination without adequate notice or refusal to honor valid life insurance or other claims
  • Disputes over the amount owed for covered losses

How the Firm Helps

Representation often begins with a careful review of the policy, claim file, and communications with the insurer. Where appropriate, the firm works through the company’s internal dispute process and may involve the Texas Department of Insurance. If those steps do not produce a fair result, litigation may be pursued to recover covered losses as well as additional damages available under Texas law, including attorney’s fees and other consequential harms in appropriate cases.

The objective is to put the policyholder as nearly as possible in the position they would have occupied had the claim been handled correctly from the start.
