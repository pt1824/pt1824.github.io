Estate Planning & Probate

Thoughtful documents and guidance that protect your wishes and ease the process for those you leave behind.

Why Estate Planning Matters

A well-designed estate plan does more than distribute assets. It names decision-makers, reduces family conflict, can minimize taxes and court involvement, and ensures your health care and financial preferences are followed if you become unable to speak for yourself.

The Law Office of Keval Patel works with clients of varying net worth and family situations to create practical, Texas-compliant plans. Drawing on tax knowledge from earlier career experience, the firm also considers opportunities to structure arrangements efficiently.

Services Offered

  • Wills — Simple and complex wills that appoint executors, name guardians for minor children, and direct the distribution of property.
  • Revocable Living Trusts — Tools that can avoid probate for assets transferred into the trust during life, while retaining control and flexibility.
  • Testamentary and Other Trusts — Including provisions for special needs, education, or staggered distributions to beneficiaries.
  • Durable Powers of Attorney — Financial decision-making authority that can take effect immediately or upon incapacity.
  • Health Care Directives & Medical Powers of Attorney — Documents that express treatment preferences and appoint someone to make medical decisions.
  • Asset Protection Strategies — Discussion of options appropriate to the client’s circumstances and goals.
  • Probate & Estate Administration — Guidance for executors and administrators through the Texas probate process, whether or not a will exists.
  • Trust & Probate Litigation — Representation in disputes among beneficiaries or challenges involving executors or trustees.

Every plan is individualized. There is no one-size-fits-all package; documents are drafted to reflect the client’s family structure, assets, and values.

The Process

  1. Initial consultation to understand goals, family situation, and assets.
  2. Discussion of available tools and recommendations tailored to those goals.
  3. Preparation of draft documents for review.
  4. Execution of final documents with proper formalities under Texas law.
  5. Guidance on funding trusts or updating beneficiary designations where needed.

Probate Assistance

When a loved one passes, the probate process can feel overwhelming. The firm assists personal representatives with inventory, notices, creditor claims, accountings, and distribution, always with an eye toward efficiency and compliance with Texas Estates Code requirements.
