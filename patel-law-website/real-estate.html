Real Estate Law

Transactional guidance and dispute resolution for homeowners, investors, landlords, and developers in the greater Houston area.

Comprehensive Real Estate Representation

Whether you are buying your first home, managing investment properties, or facing a dispute over title, contracts, or possession, experienced counsel can reduce risk and protect your position. The Law Office of Keval Patel represents both individual and commercial clients in a wide range of real property matters.

Transactional Services

  • Review and negotiation of purchase and sale contracts
  • Assistance with residential and commercial closings
  • Title examination and curing of title defects
  • Lease drafting and negotiation for landlords and tenants
  • Construction-related agreements and issues
  • Mortgage and financing documentation review

Disputes & Litigation

  • Foreclosure defense and strategies to explore alternatives such as loan modification or refinance
  • Ownership and boundary disputes
  • Landlord-tenant matters, including security deposit claims and eviction proceedings
  • Claims involving title insurance
  • Construction defects and related contractual disputes
  • Deceptive Trade Practices Act claims arising from real estate transactions
  • Prosecution and defense of contract claims related to real property

Particular attention is given to helping homeowners facing foreclosure explore available options and hold lenders accountable for commitments made during the loan process.

Who We Serve

Homeowners, first-time buyers, experienced investors, landlords, tenants, developers, and small businesses involved in real estate transactions or disputes across Sugar Land, Fort Bend County, and the broader Houston region.
