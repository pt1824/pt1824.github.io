Who commercial monument signs are for

Businesses and organizations that want a permanent entrance landmark: office parks, storefronts, HOAs and neighborhoods, churches, schools, civic sites, medical campuses, and industrial parks around Greater Houston. Cultured stone monuments read as established and professional without the maintenance cycle of painted wood or vinyl banners.

Common projects include business-name monuments, address boulders, neighborhood entry signs, campus markers, and logo stones for rebrands or new construction. If drivers need to spot your entrance from the road, a carved monument does that job day and night.

Our process

  1. Share brand assets — logo files, approved colors (if applicable), messaging, size goals, and site photos.
  2. Approve a proof — we layout lettering and artwork so stakeholders can sign off before production.
  3. We craft the monument — cultured stone is shaped and engraved in our Houston workshop.
  4. Delivery / installation coordination — we arrange pickup or delivery and can discuss mounting for your site conditions.

Typical commercial sign projects finish in 6–10 weeks after proof approval. Multi-stone entries or complex logos may need extra time—we will confirm the schedule when we quote.

Materials built for Houston visibility

We use cultured stone engineered for Texas weather—not granite memorial slabs and not painted plywood. Engraved lettering stays readable through heat, humidity, and storms with far less upkeep than painted metal or wood. The result is a landmark that still looks intentional years later.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a commercial monument sign take?

Most projects complete in about 6–10 weeks after you approve the proof. Complex logos, multi-stone entries, or HOA approvals can extend the timeline.

Can you include our company logo?

Yes. Provide a clean logo file (vector preferred) and we incorporate branding into the proof for approval before we build.

Do you work with HOAs and property managers?

Yes. Neighborhood entry monuments and campus markers are common. We can work from your brand guidelines and coordinate with your decision-makers on proofs.

Why cultured stone for a business entrance?

Cultured stone looks permanent, handles Houston weather, and keeps lettering readable without constant repainting. It is a practical alternative to wood and many painted metal signs.
