Houston Headstones by Bedrock Boulders designs and builds cultured stone headstones for families and funeral homes. We do not sell granite markers. Cultured stone lets us shape, color, and engrave a memorial that looks like natural stone and holds up in Houston heat and humidity.

Cultured stone vs granite. Granite is quarried stone and often costs more and takes longer. Our product is custom cultured stone, with more flexibility for shapes and colors and a typical 6–10 week timeline.

What we make. Upright and custom-shaped memorials, matching bases, professional lettering, optional ceramic photos with a lifetime warranty against fading, and matching stone vases. Use the online designer to preview a layout, then we proof the design with you.

Price and timeline. Custom headstones start at $899, including a matching base and engraving. Typical completion is 6–10 weeks. We do not publish other package prices online; contact us for a quote on size, color, photos, and cemetery requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are your headstones granite or cultured stone?
They are cultured stone, not granite. Cultured stone is a durable, weather-resistant material we shape and engrave in Houston. It is typically more affordable and faster than granite, with more flexibility for custom shapes and colors.

How much does a custom headstone cost?
Custom headstones start at $899, including a matching base and engraving. Photos, vases, and fully custom shapes are quoted separately. We do not list other prices on this site.

How long does a headstone take?
Typical projects are completed and delivered in 6–10 weeks, depending on design and cemetery requirements.

Do I need an appointment? What are your hours?
Yes. Visits are by appointment. We are open Sunday through Saturday, 9:00am to 6:00pm. Call (281) 901-0788 before coming. We are not a walk-in retail shop.

Which Houston-area cemeteries and cities do you serve?
We serve Houston, Sugar Land, Rosenberg, Richmond, Needville, and the greater Houston area. We help families meet cemetery size and installation rules and can coordinate with the cemetery.

Do you offer custom shapes or designs?
Yes. We offer standard sizes for simplicity and can create custom shapes, sizes, and colors.

Do you work with funeral homes?
Yes. We work with funeral homes throughout Texas on affordable cultured stone headstones.

Do your ceramic photos last?
Yes. Our ceramic photos include a lifetime warranty against fading and weather damage.
