Headstone Prices in Houston TX: 2026 Guide

March 25, 2026

Prices for custom headstones in the Houston area vary based on size, style, material, engraving complexity, and cemetery requirements. As a starting point for 2026:

  • Single upright or standard markers often begin around $899–$1,299
  • Companion (double) headstones typically start near $1,599–$1,999
  • Flat markers and smaller tablets can start lower
  • Memorial benches commonly begin around $1,499–$1,799

These are starting ranges. Final cost depends on exact size, number of photos or vases, special artwork, and delivery or installation needs. We always provide a written quote and a free design proof before any deposit is requested so families can decide with clear information.

The biggest cost drivers are usually size, complexity of engraving, and any special cemetery foundation requirements. Asking about the cemetery rules early helps keep the final number predictable.

Navigating Cemetery Regulations for Your Custom Headstone

March 1, 2025

Almost every cemetery has rules about size, height, thickness, foundation, and sometimes material or color. These rules exist for maintenance and appearance reasons, and they must be followed for the memorial to be installed without problems.

When you share the name of the cemetery with us, we can often confirm the main requirements and design within those limits from the beginning. This simple step prevents the frustration of a beautiful design that later needs expensive changes.

If you are unsure of the rules, we are happy to help you find them or contact the cemetery office. Taking a few minutes early protects both your time and your investment.

Designing a Headstone: 5 Key Considerations

July 15, 2025

Creating a custom headstone is a meaningful process. Five practical points we discuss with most families:

  1. Material and durability — Choose something that will hold up to Houston heat, humidity, and rain for decades.
  2. Personalization — Names, dates, a short inscription, symbols, or a photo that feel right for the person being honored.
  3. Cemetery rules — Size, height, and foundation requirements must be met.
  4. Installation and stability — Proper base or footer work keeps the memorial level and secure.
  5. Budget and timeline — Clear pricing and a realistic production window (typically 6–10 weeks after approval) reduce stress.

We guide families through these points calmly so the final memorial feels both personal and practical.

Personalizing Your Headstone: Symbols and Engravings

April 5, 2025

Symbols and carefully chosen words help a memorial tell a story. Common choices include crosses, flowers, military emblems, hearts, praying hands, or simple decorative borders. Short phrases such as “In Loving Memory,” “Forever in Our Hearts,” or a favorite Bible verse are also frequently used.

We help families decide what feels meaningful without overcrowding the design. Clean lettering and balanced layout keep the memorial readable and dignified for years to come.

How to Maintain Your Headstone for Long-Lasting Beauty

May 10, 2025

Polished granite is naturally durable, but a little care keeps it looking its best. In Houston’s climate we recommend:

  • Gentle cleaning with water and a soft cloth or non-abrasive brush when needed
  • Avoiding harsh chemicals or pressure washers
  • Checking the base or footing occasionally to make sure it remains level
  • Clearing grass or debris that can hold moisture against the stone

Most families find that occasional light cleaning is all that is required for the memorial to stay clear and respectful for generations.

Affordable Headstones Without Sacrificing Quality

February 15, 2025

Honoring a loved one does not have to mean choosing the most expensive option. Starting prices for solid, well-made memorials can begin under $1,000 for simpler markers. The keys to staying within budget while still getting quality are:

  • Clear communication about size and features from the beginning
  • Choosing designs that fit the cemetery rules the first time
  • Using a free design proof so changes happen on paper, not after production
  • Working with a local shop that keeps delivery and coordination simple

We believe every family deserves a respectful, lasting memorial at a price that feels fair. We are happy to walk through options openly so you can decide what fits your situation.
