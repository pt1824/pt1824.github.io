A Houston Family Business Focused on Lasting Memorials

Houston Headstones is the memorial division of Bedrock Boulders. We create custom polished granite headstones and markers for families across the Houston area and beyond.

We began with custom engraved stone signs for ranches, homes, and businesses. Over time, more families asked us for headstones. We listened. Today our focus is on high-quality granite memorials — pieces that honor a life with dignity and stand the test of Texas weather for generations.

We are not a large national company. We are local. That means you speak with people who understand Houston cemeteries, local requirements, and the importance of personal care during a difficult time.

What Guides Our Work

Empathy First

Every order represents a life and a family. We take time to listen, never rush decisions, and treat every project with quiet respect.

Quality Granite

We work with durable polished granite chosen for color consistency, polish quality, and long-term resistance to Houston heat, rain, and humidity.

Sustainable Pace

As a side-business rooted in family values, we take on work we can do well without sacrificing quality or personal well-being. Honest timelines matter.

Serving Houston & Beyond

We regularly work with families and funeral homes in the greater Houston area, including surrounding communities. We understand common cemetery regulations and can help coordinate installation or delivery.

Many of our clients find us through Google, recommendations from funeral directors, or previous residential and commercial stone work. We also welcome partnerships with funeral homes and cemeteries that value reliable local craftsmanship.
