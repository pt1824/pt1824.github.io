Companion & Double Granite Headstones in Houston

Side-by-side designs for couples starting at $1,999. Free custom design proof within one business day. Local family-owned service for Houston, Katy, Sugar Land and surrounding areas.

Who This Is For

Companion (double) headstones are designed for couples who want to be remembered together. They provide two panels or a continuous design with space for both names, dates, and shared or individual epitaphs.

Families often choose them when planning ahead or when one spouse has already passed and the second panel is left for the future. They create a unified, lasting memorial.

Starting at $1,999 including strong matching base, custom lettering, free design proof, and clear written quote.

What Problem It Solves

Separate single headstones can feel disconnected. A companion monument keeps the couple together visually and simplifies future decisions for the surviving spouse or family.

Service Area

Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, The Woodlands, Pearland, Richmond, Rosenberg and nearby communities. We coordinate with local cemetery requirements.

Strong & Lasting

Thicker polished granite and a solid matching base built for Texas weather. Designed to remain stable and beautiful for generations.

What’s Included

  • Side-by-side polished granite design
  • Strong matching base
  • Two names, dates, and shared or individual text
  • Free design proof within 1 business day
  • Optional photos, vases, symbols
  • Flexible layout (one continuous panel or two)

  • Installation coordination
