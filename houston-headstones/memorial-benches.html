Granite Memorial Benches in Houston

Polished granite memorial benches starting at $1,799. Engraved backrest for names, dates, or messages. Suitable for cemeteries, gardens, or parks. Free design proof. Local family-owned.

Who This Is For

Memorial benches provide a place to sit and remember. They work well in cemeteries that allow them, in family plots, or as lasting tributes in gardens, parks, schools, or private property.

Families choose them when they want something functional as well as commemorative — a quiet place to rest and reflect.

Starting at $1,799 for a polished granite seat and back with engraved backrest, free design proof, and written quote.

What Problem It Solves

Traditional headstones mark the place; a bench invites people to stay. It is especially meaningful for families who visit often or want a shared gathering spot.

Service Area

Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, The Woodlands, Pearland and surrounding areas. We can discuss placement rules with the cemetery or property owner.

Built to Last Outdoors

Solid polished granite designed for continuous outdoor exposure in the Houston climate. Durable finish that requires little maintenance.
