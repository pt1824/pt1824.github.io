Flat Granite Markers in Houston

Affordable polished flat markers and flush tablets starting at $899. Free design proof within one business day. Ideal for many Houston-area cemeteries. Local family-owned service.

Who This Is For

Flat markers (also called flush or grass markers) sit level with the ground. They are required or preferred by many cemeteries, cost less than uprights, and still allow meaningful personalization with names, dates, and simple designs.

Families choose them when the cemetery has height restrictions, when budget is a priority, or when they prefer a clean, low-profile look that is easy to maintain.

Starting at $899 for a standard polished granite tablet with name, dates, simple text, and free design proof.

What Problem It Solves

Cemetery rules often limit upright monuments. Flat markers meet those rules while still giving a permanent, dignified marker. They are also the most affordable polished granite option we offer.

Service Area

Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, The Woodlands, Pearland, and surrounding communities. We work with cemetery requirements across Greater Houston.

Durable & Practical

Thick polished granite that resists Texas weather. Optional vase. Easy mowing and maintenance for the cemetery and family.

What’s Included
