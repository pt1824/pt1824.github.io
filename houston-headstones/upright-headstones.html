Upright Granite Headstones in Houston

Classic polished upright memorials starting at $1,299. Free custom design proof within one business day. Local family-owned service for Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress and surrounding communities.

Who This Is For

Upright headstones are the traditional choice for families who want a lasting, visible memorial. They stand above the ground, offer more surface area for names, dates, epitaphs, symbols, and photos, and create a strong focal point in the cemetery.

We serve families and funeral homes throughout Greater Houston who need a durable polished granite upright that meets local cemetery rules and stands up to Texas heat, humidity, and storms.

Starting at $1,299 including base, custom lettering, free design proof, and written quote before any deposit.

What Problem It Solves

Many families want something more substantial than a flat marker but worry about cost, timeline, and cemetery approval. Our uprights give you presence and personalization without the high prices or long waits of traditional monument yards.

Service Area

Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, The Woodlands, Pearland, Richmond, Rosenberg, and surrounding areas. We coordinate delivery and installation with most local cemeteries.

Built for Houston
