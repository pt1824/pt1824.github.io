What Happens After You Send the Design

We review your layout, refine the lettering and spacing, check against typical cemetery regulations, and return a polished digital proof. You can request changes until everything feels right. Only then do we discuss final pricing and production.

Typical production time after final approval is 6–10 weeks. No deposit is required for the first proof.
