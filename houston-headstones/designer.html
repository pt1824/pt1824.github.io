Free Headstone Designer

Design your custom headstone online in minutes. Choose the layout, add text, upload a photo, and download a proof. We will refine it into a professional design within 24 hours.

How to Use the Online Designer

  1. Open the free designer tool on our main site (or describe what you want here).
  2. Click any headstone shape, text, or symbol, then drag the corner handles to resize or move it.
  3. Use the tools: Add Text, Upload Photo, Delete Selected, or Reset Design.
  4. When you are happy with the layout, click Download PNG.
  5. Email the PNG file to main@bedrockboulders.net or submit it through our contact form.

We will turn your rough design into a clean professional proof and send it back, usually within one business day. No deposit is required for the first proof.

Prefer to talk it through?

You do not have to use the online tool. Call or message us with the names, dates, preferred color, and any special details. We will create the first proof for you.

What Happens After You Send the Design

We review your layout, clean up the lettering and proportions, check against typical cemetery requirements, and return a polished digital proof. You can request changes until everything feels right. Only then do we discuss pricing and production.

Typical production time after final approval is 6–10 weeks.
