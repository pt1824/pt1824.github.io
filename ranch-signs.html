Who ranch signs are for

Property owners who want a permanent entrance marker—not a temporary wood board that warps after one summer. Typical customers include working ranches, horse farms, weekend acreage, hunting leases, and rural homesteads across the Houston metro and surrounding counties.

If guests, delivery drivers, or emergency services need to find your gate fast, a carved address boulder is both practical and personal. Families often combine a ranch name with a street number so the entrance works as wayfinding and as a statement piece.

Our process

  1. Share the idea — ranch name, logo, address, size preferences, and photos of the gate or driveway.
  2. Approve a proof — we layout lettering and artwork so you can review spelling and style before production.
  3. We craft the stone — cultured stone is shaped and engraved in our Houston workshop.
  4. Delivery / placement — we coordinate pickup or delivery and can discuss mounting options for your site.

Most projects complete in 6–10 weeks after proof approval. Complex logos or oversized monuments may take longer; we will set expectations up front.

Materials that handle Texas weather

We use cultured stone—not painted wood and not granite memorial stock. Cultured stone is cost-effective, durable, and designed for Texas heat, humidity, and storms. Engraved lettering stays readable without constant touch-ups. Unlike many painted signs, it resists peeling, fading, and rot.

We combine design software with hands-on craftsmanship so your ranch name looks intentional on the stone, not stamped as an afterthought.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a ranch entrance sign take?

Most cultured stone ranch signs finish in about 6–10 weeks after you approve the design proof. Custom logos, larger stones, or installation coordination can adjust the timeline.

What can you engrave on a ranch boulder?

Ranch names, family names, established dates, street numbers, brands/logos, and short mottos are common. You approve a proof before we build.

Do you serve ranches outside Houston?

Yes. We build for Houston-area and greater Texas properties. Call to discuss delivery and placement for your gate.

Why choose cultured stone instead of wood or painted metal?

Cultured stone holds up to Texas sun and humidity better than painted wood and does not peel like many metal finishes. Engraved lettering stays readable with minimal upkeep.
