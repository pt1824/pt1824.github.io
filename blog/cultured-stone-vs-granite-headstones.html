Cultured Stone vs Granite Headstones in Houston

Posted on October 12, 2026 | By Bedrock Boulders Team

Short answer: Bedrock Boulders makes custom cultured stone headstones, not granite. Cultured stone usually costs less (ours start at $899), is typically ready in 6–10 weeks, and offers more shapes and colors; granite is a traditional quarried stone that often costs more and can take longer.


What is cultured stone?

Cultured stone is a durable, weather-resistant material that we shape, color, and engrave in our Houston workshop. It looks like natural stone but is made to order, which gives more flexibility for custom shapes and colors. It is not granite, and we say that clearly so families can compare honestly.

When cultured stone makes sense

Families choose cultured stone when they want a custom look, a faster local timeline, and a lower starting price. See our Houston headstone prices 2026 guide for typical price ranges; every headstone is quoted individually.


Check your cemetery's rules first

Cemetery rules on size, material, finish, and installation vary. Before you order any headstone, ask your cemetery for its guidelines. Tell us the cemetery name and we will help you stay within them and can coordinate with cemetery staff. Read more in our cemetery regulations guide.


Frequently asked questions

Are your headstones granite?

No. Bedrock Boulders makes custom cultured stone headstones, not granite. Cultured stone is shaped, colored, and engraved in our Houston workshop.

Is cultured stone cheaper than granite?

Usually, yes. Our cultured stone headstones start at $899 with a matching base and engraving, while granite uprights in the Houston market often start in the $2,000–$4,000+ range before extras.

How long does a cultured stone headstone take?

Most of our memorials are completed and delivered in 6–10 weeks after the design is approved. Imported granite can involve longer lead times.

Will my cemetery accept a cultured stone headstone?

Rules vary by cemetery, so check with your cemetery first. Tell us the cemetery name and we help you work within its size, material, and installation rules and can coordinate with cemetery staff.

Can I add a photo to a cultured stone headstone?

Yes. We offer ceramic photos with a lifetime warranty against fading and weather damage, plus matching stone vases.
